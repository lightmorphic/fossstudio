Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to read this site and use the software it describes. The target for fossstudio.org is WCAG 2.1 level AA.

What's in place

  • Light and dark themes, both contrast-checked, following your system setting until you choose
  • Full keyboard operation, visible focus outlines, and a skip-to-content link
  • Your reduced-motion preference is honoured: animations stop, and the playable game waits for a deliberate press instead of auto-starting
  • One self-hosted font with system fallbacks, real text throughout, and images with alternative text

Known limits

The waiting-room game demo is drawn on a canvas, so its play isn't available to screen readers. It's optional entertainment, labelled as such, and nothing on the site depends on it. The FOSSStudio application itself is a live video tool and parts of it are inherently visual; we keep improving its labelling and keyboard support release by release.

Tell us what's in the way

If anything on this site is hard to use with your setup, email complaints@fossstudio.org and tell us what happened and what you were using. Fixing it goes to the top of the list.
